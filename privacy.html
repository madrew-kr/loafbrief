Privacy policy

Loaf Brief Publisher · last updated 2026-11-25

Who uses this app

Loaf Brief Publisher is used only by the owner of the 식빵 브리핑 (@loafbrief) YouTube channel to upload that channel's own videos. It is not offered to the public.

Google user data we access

  • YouTube upload (youtube.upload): to upload approved videos to the owner's channel.
  • YouTube manage (youtube.force-ssl): to set the thumbnail and captions of those videos and to confirm which channel is signed in.

How the data is used and stored

The app runs on the owner's own computer. The OAuth token is stored only there, in a file readable by the owner alone. The app reads no other account data, does not sell or share data with anyone, and does not use it for advertising or for training AI models.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What we do not collect

The app has one user: the channel owner. It does not collect, store or process data of viewers, commenters, subscribers or any other YouTube user. It does not show Google user data to anyone. Public video statistics (views, likes) of our own channel are read only to measure our own results.

Retention and deletion

The OAuth token stays on the owner's computer until the owner deletes it or revokes access. After revocation the app stops working; nothing else is kept.

YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's data handling is described in the Google Privacy Policy. See also our Terms of Service.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the local token file removes the stored token.
